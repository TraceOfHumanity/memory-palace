# Слова, які легко сплутати (Confusing Words)

## 1. make чи do

| **make** (створювати, робити результат) | **do** (діяти, виконувати) |
| --- | --- |
| make a decision / a mistake / a plan / a choice | do homework / housework / the dishes / the shopping |
| make money / a profit / a living | do business / work / a job |
| make a call / a noise / an effort / progress | do your best / a favour / good / harm / damage |
| make food: make breakfast / tea / a cake | do exercise / sport / yoga / a course |
| make friends / a suggestion / an appointment | do the washing / the cleaning / your hair |
| make sure / sense / a difference / a point | do research / an exam / a test (BrE: take) |

**do** — загальна діяльність («що-небудь»): What are you **doing**? **make** — створення чогось конкретного.

## 2. say, tell, speak, talk

| Слово | Структура | Приклад |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **say** | say **something** (+ to someone) | He **said** hello. She **said** that she was tired. |
| **tell** | tell **someone** (something) | He **told** me a story. Tell me the truth. |
| **speak** | мова, формально, монолог | She **speaks** three languages. May I **speak** to Mr Smith? |
| **talk** | розмова, неформально | We **talked** for hours. Talk **to / with** someone **about** something. |

Усталене: **tell** a lie / the truth / a story / a joke / the time / the difference; **say** goodbye / yes / a prayer; **speak** English / the truth.

## 3. look, see, watch

| | Значення |
| --- | --- |
| **see** | бачити (мимоволі) | I **see** a bird. |
| **look (at)** | дивитись (цілеспрямовано) | **Look at** this! |
| **watch** | спостерігати за рухом | **watch** TV, **watch** a film, **watch** the kids |

Також: **hear** (чути) / **listen (to)** (слухати). **notice** (помітити).

## 4. bring, take, fetch, carry
- **bring** — сюди (до мовця): **Bring** me the book. **take** — звідси: **Take** the book to school.
- **fetch** — піти й принести; **carry** — нести (тримати в руках).

## 5. borrow, lend
- **borrow** (позичити **в когось**) — I **borrowed** a pen **from** Tom.
- **lend** (позичити **комусь**) — Tom **lent** me a pen.

## 6. learn, study, teach
- **learn** — навчатись, вивчити (результат): I'm **learning** English.
- **study** — вивчати (процес, предмет): She **studies** medicine.
- **teach** — навчати: He **teaches** maths.

## 7. rise, raise, arise; lie, lay; sit, set
| Неперех. (без додатка) | Перех. (з додатком) |
| --- | --- |
| rise — rose — risen (підніматись) | raise — raised — raised (піднімати щось) |
| lie — lay — lain (лежати) | lay — laid — laid (класти) |
| sit — sat — sat (сидіти) | set — set — set (поставити) |
**lie** (брехати) — lied — lied.

## 8. win, beat; earn, gain; affect, effect
- **win** a game/prize; **beat** a person/team: We **beat** them and **won** the match.
- **earn** (заробляти) money; **gain** (набувати) experience; **win** a prize.
- **affect** (дієслово, впливати) · **effect** (іменник, вплив): The weather **affects** my mood. The **effect** was strong.

## 12. Типові помилки

| ❌ Неправильно | ✅ Правильно | Чому |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I made my homework. | I did my homework. | do homework |
| She did a mistake. | She made a mistake. | make a mistake |
| He said me. | He told me. | tell + особа |
| She speaks him. | She talks to him. | talk to |
| I borrowed him a book. | I lent him a book. | lend |
| I lent a pen from Tom. | I borrowed a pen from Tom. | borrow from |
| I'm interesting in music. | I'm interested in music. | -ed |
| Your welcome. | You're welcome. | you're |
| It's colour is red. | Its colour is red. | its |
| more then | more than | than |
| I loose my keys. | I lose my keys. | lose |
| a magazine to buy bread | a shop / supermarket | false friend |

---

## 13. Алгоритм

1. **Не перекладай слово в слово**: шукай **колокацію** (make a decision, do homework).
2. Для пар схожих слів — перевір значення в словнику **з прикладом**.
3. **Звертай увагу на структуру:** tell + особа; say + to; borrow from; lend to.
4. Вчи **homophones парами** в реченні.
5. Для **фальшивих друзів** — згадуй: не те, що в українській.
6. Веди **список «моїх» помилок** і повторюй.

## 14. Вправи

1. make / do: ___ homework, ___ a decision, ___ a mistake, ___ the dishes, ___ friends.
2. say / tell / speak / talk: He ___ me a story. / She ___ three languages. / He ___ goodbye.
3. borrow / lend: Can I ___ your pen? / Can you ___ me your pen?
4. their / there / they're: ___ going to ___ house. / ___ is a book.
5. Фальшиві друзі: magazine, actual, fabric, lecture, decade.

**Відповіді:**
1. do, make, make, do, make
2. told; speaks; said
3. borrow; lend
4. They're, their; There
5. журнал, справжній, тканина, лекція, десятиліття
