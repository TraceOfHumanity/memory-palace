# Непряма мова (Reported Speech)

**Пряма мова** (Direct Speech) — слова людини дослівно, в лапках: *She said, "I am tired."*
**Непряма мова** (Reported Speech) — переказ слів без лапок: *She said (that) she was tired.*

> Головна ідея: переказуємо слова **з позиції зараз**, тому **час «відступає» назад (backshift)**, а займенники та вказівки на час і місце змінюються.

---

## 1. Backshift — зсув часів

Якщо **дієслово переказу в минулому** (said, told, asked), то часи зсуваються на крок у минуле:

| Пряма мова | Непряма мова |
| --- | --- |
| Present Simple: "I **work**." | Past Simple: He said he **worked**. |
| Present Continuous: "I **am working**." | Past Continuous: He said he **was working**. |
| Present Perfect: "I **have worked**." | Past Perfect: He said he **had worked**. |
| Past Simple: "I **worked**." | Past Perfect: He said he **had worked**. |
| Past Continuous: "I **was working**." | Past Perfect Continuous: He said he **had been working**. |
| Past Perfect: "I **had worked**." | **не змінюється**: He said he **had worked**. |
| will: "I **will** work." | would: He said he **would** work. |
| can: "I **can** work." | could: He said he **could** work. |
| may: "I **may** work." | might: He said he **might** work. |
| must: "I **must** work." | had to: He said he **had to** work. |
| shall: "Shall I work?" | should / would |
| **не змінюються:** would, could, should, might, ought to | He said he **would/could/should/might** work. |

### Коли зсуву НЕМАЄ
1. **Дієслово переказу в теперішньому:** She **says** she is tired. · He has said he will come.
2. **Факт, який досі правда:** She said that the Earth **goes** around the Sun. · He told me he **lives** in Kyiv (і досі живе).
3. Тільки Past Perfect і модальні would, could, should, might, ought to.

---

## 2. Інші зміни

### Займенники — за змістом
He said, "**I** like **my** job." → He said (that) **he** liked **his** job.
She said to me, "**You** are late." → She told me **I** was late.

### Вказівки на час і місце

| Пряма | Непряма |
| --- | --- |
| now | then / at that moment |
| today | that day |
| tonight | that night |
| yesterday | the day before / the previous day |
| tomorrow | the next day / the following day |
| last week / year | the week / year before |
| next week / year | the following week / year |
| ago | before |
| this | that |
| these | those |
| here | there |
| come | go (за перспективою) |

## 3. Типи речень

### 3.1. Розповідні (statements)
**say / tell**: 
- **said (that) + clause**: He **said (that)** he was tired.
- **told + особа + (that)**: He **told me (that)** he was tired.
- ❌ He said me. ❌ He told that he was tired. (tell потребує особи)
- **said to me** = told me.
**that** можна пропускати.

### 3.2. Загальні запитання (yes/no) — **if / whether**
"Do you like coffee?" → She **asked** me **if / whether** I liked coffee.
- **порядок слів прямий** (підмет перед дієсловом);
- **без do/does/did** та без знака питання.
❌ She asked me did I like coffee. ✅ She asked me if I liked coffee.

### 3.3. Wh-запитання — питальне слово зберігається, порядок прямий
"Where do you live?" → He **asked** me **where I lived**.
"What is she doing?" → I asked **what she was doing**.
"When will they arrive?" → He asked **when they would arrive**.
❌ He asked where was I. ✅ He asked where I was.

### 3.4. Накази й прохання — **tell / ask + особа + (not) to + V1**
"Close the door!" → He **told me to close** the door.
"Please help me." → She **asked me to help** her.
"Don't be late." → He **told me not to be** late.
"Don't worry." → She asked me **not to worry**.

### 3.5. Пропозиції та поради
"Let's go." → He **suggested going**. / He **suggested (that) we go**.
"Why don't you rest?" → She suggested (that) I rest. / suggested resting.
"I'll help you." → He **offered to help** me. · He **promised to help**.
"You should go." → He **advised me to go**.
"Don't touch it!" → He **warned me not to touch** it.

---

## 4. Дієслова переказу (reporting verbs)

| Структура | Дієслова |
| --- | --- |
| + that-clause | say, tell, explain, announce, admit, complain, mention, add, answer, reply |
| + особа + to + V1 | tell, ask, order, advise, warn, remind, invite, allow, encourage, persuade |
| + to + V1 | promise, offer, refuse, agree, threaten, decide |
| + V-ing | suggest, deny, admit, recommend, apologize for, insist on |
| + if/whether | ask, wonder, want to know |

"I didn't do it." → He **denied doing** it. · He **admitted breaking** the window.
"I'm sorry I'm late." → She **apologized for being** late.

---

## 5. Типові помилки

| ❌ Неправильно | ✅ Правильно | Чому |
| --- | --- | --- |
| He said me that he was tired. | He told me that he was tired. | said без особи / told з особою |
| He told that he was tired. | He said that he was tired. | told потребує особи |
| He asked where was I. | He asked where I was. | прямий порядок |
| She asked me did I like it. | She asked me if I liked it. | if / whether |
| He asked me do I know. | He asked me if I knew. | без do |
| He told me close the door. | He told me to close the door. | to + V1 |
| He said me to go. | He told me to go. | tell |
| She said she is tired (минулий переказ). | She said she was tired. | backshift |
| He asked what is she doing. | He asked what she was doing. | прямий порядок + backshift |
| She said that she will come tomorrow. | She said that she would come the next day. | would + the next day |
| He suggested me to go. | He suggested going. / that I go. | suggest + -ing |
| She said that don't worry. | She asked me not to worry. | наказ → ask / tell + not to |

---

## 6. Алгоритм

1. Визнач тип: **розповідь**, **питання**, **наказ/прохання**.
2. Дієслово переказу **в минулому**? → зроби **backshift**. (В теперішньому — не зсувай.)
3. Зміни **займенники** за змістом.
4. Зміни **вказівки** на час і місце (now → then, tomorrow → the next day).
5. **Розповідь:** said (that) / told me (that) + clause.
6. **Yes/no питання:** asked if / whether + підмет + дієслово (без do).
7. **Wh-питання:** asked + питальне слово + **прямий порядок**.
8. **Наказ:** told / asked + особа + (not) to + V1.

---

## 7. Вправи

1. Перекажіть: He said, "I am tired." / She said, "I have finished." / He said, "I will call you tomorrow."
2. Питання: "Do you like tea?" / "Where do you live?" / "What are you doing?"
3. Накази: "Close the door!" / "Don't be late."
4. Знайдіть помилку: He said me that… / He asked where was I. / He told close the door.
5. Перекладіть: «Він сказав, що втомився». «Вона запитала, де я живу». «Він попросив мене не хвилюватися».

**Відповіді:**
1. He said he was tired. / She said she had finished. / He said he would call me the next day.
2. She asked if I liked tea. / She asked where I lived. / She asked what I was doing.
3. He told me to close the door. / He told me not to be late.
4. He told me that… / He asked where I was. / He told me to close the door.
5. He said (that) he was tired. / She asked where I lived. / He asked me not to worry.
